1A. Independent layout points

X1 through X5 are field layout targets. They stay editable even while the drawing scale is locked. Select an X and ATLAS shows the two tape distances from Station A and Station B to that same coordinate. SHIFT: while placing X2–X5, hold Shift to force the new point horizontal or vertical (orthogonal) to the previous X.

iPad: turn SHIFT ORTHO on to imitate holding the keyboard Shift key. X2–X5 will lock horizontal or vertical to the previous X until you turn it off.

2. Station coordinates (feet)

When enabled, A and B snap to nearby drawing geometry. ATLAS checks endpoints / extracted points and also the nearest point along a LINE or polyline segment, so a station can be placed anywhere along a drawn wall or control line. Uses the Snap radius above, in feet. Uncheck to place a station freely on the floor.
